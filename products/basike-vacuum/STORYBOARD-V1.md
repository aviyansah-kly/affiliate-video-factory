# BASIKE V1 — Editorial Affiliate Video

**Format:** 1080×1920 / 9:16  
**Target:** ~24s  
**Mode:** zero-budget validation  
**Voice:** neutral Indonesian, conversational, slightly curious  
**Visual principle:** viewer problem → product → proof/claim → use case → value → quiet CTA

## Beat 01 — Recognition / 0:00–0:03

**VO:** “Debu kecil di sela keyboard, jok mobil, atau sudut rumah itu paling gampang bikin malas bersihin.”

**Hero:** dust + tight-space problem abstraction.

**Visual:** mostly monochrome, large macro grid suggesting keyboard/crevice. Dust particles move across frame. No product yet.

**Motion:** fast reveal in first ~0.5s, then settle. The viewer should understand the problem before seeing the product.

**Avoid:** generic vacuum icon, stock cleaning footage, promo card.

## Beat 02 — Product reveal / 0:03–0:06

**VO:** “Ini BASIKE, vacuum genggam yang bentuknya memang dibuat buat area kecil.”

**Hero:** supplied BASIKE product image.

**Visual:** crop the product large and let it enter diagonally. Product is the only dominant object. Brand name small.

**Motion:** controlled parallax + subtle rotation; no bouncing UI.

**Asset:** `assets/product-hero.png`

## Beat 03 — Claim / 0:06–0:10

**VO:** “Di listing-nya, BASIKE mengklaim daya hisap sampai 9000Pa.”

**Hero:** `9000Pa`.

**Visual:** giant type takes ~65% of the visual field. Product silhouette/details remain secondary. Small source label: `Klaim pada listing`.

**Motion:** number lands exactly around the spoken “9000Pa”; use word cue when timestamped VO is available.

**Important:** Do not present 9000Pa as independently verified fact.

## Beat 04 — Use case / 0:10–0:15

**VO:** “Bisa dipakai di rumah atau mobil, dan desain pegangannya dibuat nyaman saat dipakai.”

**Hero:** real supplied product imagery.

**Visual:** diagonal split. One side suggests home/desk; the other suggests car/interior. Briefly reveal ergonomic hand-grip photo rather than inventing a cleaning result.

**Motion:** two images cross through the diagonal seam; one controlled push-in only.

**Avoid:** fake dirt disappearing, fake suction particles, fake testimonial.

## Beat 05 — Value / 0:15–0:19

**VO:** “Harganya saat saya cek sekitar Rp98 ribuan.”

**Hero:** `Rp98 ribuan`.

**Visual:** editorial price poster. Lots of breathing room. Tiny caption: `harga saat pengecekan · dapat berubah`.

**Motion:** price enters as one strong typographic move, not a marketplace discount badge.

## Beat 06 — Close / 0:19–0:24

**VO:** “Kalau tertarik, jangan cuma lihat harganya—cek spesifikasi dan review produknya dulu.”

**Hero:** BASIKE product image.

**Visual:** product on right; CTA copy on left. Background returns to the visual language from Beat 01.

**CTA:** `Cek spesifikasi + review`

**Motion:** calm finish; no flashing button, countdown, fake urgency, or multiple CTAs.

## QA gates

- First ~2 seconds communicate a recognizable problem.
- No two consecutive beats use the same framing.
- Every beat has one dominant hero.
- Product identity uses supplied real imagery.
- 9000Pa is attributed to the listing.
- Price is time-qualified because marketplace prices can change.
- No fabricated cleaning performance.
- No fabricated testimonial/review.
- No generic AI presenter.
- No marketplace-card visual language.
- Final CTA is singular and low-pressure.

## V2 upgrade path

If V1 proves that the visual system is worth scaling, add paid generation only to the hook or another beat where photoreal footage creates a real emotional difference. Keep product facts, typography, transitions, and layout programmatic so revisions remain cheap.
